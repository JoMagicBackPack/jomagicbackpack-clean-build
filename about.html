About the Pack

JoMagicBackpack is a menagerie of finds: part vintage, part new, always surprising. Each piece is chosen because it has character, usefulness, humor, beauty, or some odd little spark that makes it worth noticing.

The pack is not built around one strict theme. It is built around curiosity — the useful, the strange, the beautiful, the funny, and the overlooked things that still have life in them.
