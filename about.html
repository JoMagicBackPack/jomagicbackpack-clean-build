About the Pack

JoMagicBackpack is a wandering collection of strange, useful, beautiful, nostalgic, and overlooked things. Some are vintage. Some are modern. Some are difficult to explain. All of them earned their way into the pack somehow.

The idea is simple: if something sparks curiosity, humor, wonder, usefulness, or memory — it belongs here. The backpack became less of a logo over time and more of a symbol for carrying pieces of the world that still feel alive.

Part thrift expedition. Part curiosity cabinet. Part digital backpack carried from one strange find to the next.
